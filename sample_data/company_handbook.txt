Employee Leave Policy

All full-time employees are entitled to 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
Leave requests must be submitted at least 3 working days in advance through the HR portal.
Unused leave can be carried forward up to a maximum of 5 days into the next calendar year.
Sick leave is granted separately, up to 10 days per year, and does not require advance notice
but must be reported to the manager on the day of absence.

Work From Home Policy

Employees may work from home up to 2 days per week, subject to manager approval.
Remote work requests should be submitted through the internal portal at least 1 day in advance.
Employees working remotely are expected to be available during core hours, 10 AM to 5 PM,
and must attend all scheduled meetings via video call.

Reimbursement Policy

Business-related expenses such as travel, client meetings, and approved software subscriptions
are reimbursable. Employees must submit receipts within 30 days of the expense through the
finance portal. Reimbursements are processed within 10 working days of approval.

Performance Review Cycle

Performance reviews are conducted twice a year, in April and October.
Each review includes a self-assessment, manager assessment, and a calibration discussion.
Promotions and salary revisions are typically processed following the April review cycle.
